## Summary of OctoAcme project management processes

OctoAcme uses an iterative, customer-focused project lifecycle. During **initiation**, the team validates the problem and business need, defines measurable outcomes, identifies stakeholders, considers initial risks and resources, and makes a go/no-go decision. During **planning**, an approved initiative becomes a prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria, estimates, a Definition of Done, dependencies, milestones, and a release plan.

During **execution and tracking**, teams deliver small increments, use the project board to make work visible, collaborate through reviewed pull requests, and maintain quality through automated and manual testing. Risks, dependencies, blockers, progress, and success metrics are monitored throughout delivery and escalated according to their impact.

For **release and deployment**, the team confirms acceptance criteria, CI and security checks, release notes, rollback or mitigation plans, and post-deployment verification before communicating the release. After each sprint, release, or significant milestone, the team conducts a **retrospective** to identify what went well, what can improve, and which actions have owners and due dates. Project Managers coordinate delivery, risks, schedules, and communication; Product Managers guide outcomes and priorities; Developers and QA support implementation and quality; and stakeholders provide context, feedback, and approvals.
