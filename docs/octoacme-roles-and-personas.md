# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises. The role definitions below clarify accountability, decision ownership, participation, and handoffs across the project lifecycle.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Executive Sponsors

### Role Summary
Executive Sponsors provide strategic direction, organizational sponsorship, and senior-level support. They own the business case and ensure that the project remains aligned with organizational priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Approve the business case, major funding decisions, and strategic objectives
- Resolve escalated impediments beyond the Project Manager's authority
- Approve significant scope, schedule, or investment changes
- Champion the project with senior stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Receive status, risk, and decision escalations from the Project Manager
- Align outcomes and priorities with Product Managers
- Work with the Steering Committee on governance decisions
- Support the team by removing organizational barriers rather than directing day-to-day delivery

---

## Steering Committees and Governance Boards

### Role Summary
Steering Committees provide cross-functional oversight and governance for projects with material risk, investment, or organizational impact.

### Responsibilities
- Review progress, dependencies, major risks, and change requests
- Resolve decisions that exceed the authority of the Project Manager or Product Manager
- Validate stage gates and continued investment
- Confirm that the project remains aligned with strategic objectives

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partner with the Executive Sponsor on strategic decisions
- Use recommendations and evidence from the Project Manager, Product Manager, and functional leads
- Provide direction without replacing the Project Manager's delivery coordination responsibilities

---

## Product Owners and Business Owners

### Role Summary
Product Owners or Business Owners represent the business outcome being delivered. They turn stakeholder needs into priorities and make timely scope and acceptance decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Prioritize requirements and backlog items
- Clarify acceptance criteria and business rules
- Make scope and priority trade-off decisions
- Accept or reject delivered outcomes against agreed criteria
- Represent user and business needs during planning and validation

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborate with Product Managers on vision, outcomes, and prioritization
- Work with Business Analysts to clarify requirements and traceability
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on scope, schedule, and decision impacts
- Provide timely feedback to Developers and Quality Assurance Leads

---

## Business Analysts and Requirements Leads

### Role Summary
Business Analysts and Requirements Leads translate business needs into clear, testable, and traceable requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document needs, processes, and business rules
- Maintain requirements traceability from objectives through acceptance
- Identify gaps, assumptions, dependencies, and impacted stakeholders
- Support estimation, validation, and change analysis

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Facilitate discovery with Product Owners, users, and subject-matter experts
- Provide clear inputs to Developers and Technical Leads
- Help the Project Manager assess the impact of proposed changes
- Partner with Quality Assurance Leads to ensure requirements are testable

---

## Technical Leads and Solution Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Solution Architects own technical direction and ensure that the solution meets functional, non-functional, security, and operational needs.

### Responsibilities
- Define and document architecture and key technical decisions
- Identify technical risks, constraints, and dependencies
- Establish non-functional requirements and engineering standards
- Review designs and guide Developers toward maintainable solutions
- Advise on technical feasibility, trade-offs, and implementation sequencing

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Provide technical estimates and risks to the Project Manager
- Translate Product Owner and Business Analyst needs into viable technical approaches
- Collaborate with Developers, Quality Assurance, Security, and Operations
- Escalate architecture decisions to governance when they affect material risk or investment

---

## Workstream and Functional Leads

### Role Summary
Workstream and Functional Leads coordinate delivery within a specialized area and maintain accountability for that workstream's outputs.

### Responsibilities
- Break down workstream deliverables and coordinate assignments
- Track progress, capacity, dependencies, and quality within the function
- Provide estimates and status updates
- Escalate resource, quality, or dependency concerns early
- Coordinate handoffs between the workstream and the broader project team

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Report delivery status and risks to the Project Manager
- Coordinate priorities with Product Managers and Product Owners
- Work with the Technical Lead, Quality Assurance Lead, and other functional leads on cross-workstream dependencies
- Support Developers and specialists without duplicating the Project Manager's overall accountability

---

## Quality Assurance and Test Leads

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance and Test Leads establish the quality strategy and provide evidence that the delivered solution is ready for acceptance or release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, scope, environments, and quality criteria
- Coordinate test planning and execution
- Track defects, quality risks, and unresolved acceptance issues
- Report quality trends and readiness recommendations
- Confirm that acceptance criteria and non-functional requirements are covered

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work with Business Analysts and Product Owners to validate requirements
- Coordinate with Developers and Technical Leads on defect resolution and testability
- Provide release-readiness evidence to the Project Manager and Release Manager
- Escalate material quality risks to governance or the Executive Sponsor when necessary

---

## Change and Adoption Leads

### Role Summary
Change and Adoption Leads prepare people and operating practices for the transition to a new product, process, or capability.

### Responsibilities
- Assess stakeholder impact and adoption risks
- Plan communications, training, enablement, and readiness activities
- Gather feedback and monitor adoption after delivery
- Coordinate change actions with operational and business stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Align messages and sponsorship activities with the Executive Sponsor
- Coordinate timing and stakeholder communications with the Project Manager
- Use Product Owner and Business Analyst insights to tailor adoption activities
- Partner with the Operations or Service Owner on transition and support readiness

---

## Release and Deployment Managers

### Role Summary
Release and Deployment Managers coordinate the controlled movement of validated work into production or operational use.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain release plans, deployment schedules, and readiness checklists
- Coordinate dependencies, approvals, environments, and rollback plans
- Facilitate release readiness reviews
- Track deployment outcomes and handoff actions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Use evidence from Quality Assurance and Technical Leads to assess readiness
- Coordinate scope and timing with the Product Owner and Project Manager
- Partner with Operations or Service Owners on support and transition
- Escalate unresolved readiness risks to governance before deployment

---

## Risk, Compliance, and Security Partners

### Role Summary
Risk, Compliance, and Security Partners protect the project from unacceptable regulatory, privacy, security, control, and operational exposure.

### Responsibilities
- Identify applicable regulatory, privacy, security, and control requirements
- Review designs, plans, and releases for material exposure
- Record findings, required mitigations, and approval conditions
- Escalate risks that exceed agreed thresholds

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Maintain risk and control alignment with the Project Manager
- Advise the Technical Lead and Developers on secure and compliant implementation
- Coordinate with Business Analysts on requirements and evidence
- Provide independent input to the Product Owner, Executive Sponsor, and governance bodies

---

## Operations and Service Owners

### Role Summary
Operations and Service Owners accept responsibility for running and supporting the delivered capability after transition.

### Responsibilities
- Define support, service-level, monitoring, and operational-readiness expectations
- Confirm that support teams, runbooks, access, and monitoring are prepared
- Accept operational handoff and track early-life support issues
- Own ongoing service health after project closure

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Define operational requirements with Product Owners, Business Analysts, and Technical Leads
- Work with Release and Deployment Managers on readiness and handoff
- Coordinate incident and support readiness with Developers and Quality Assurance
- Provide operational feedback to the Project Manager during transition and retrospective activities

---

## Vendor and Partner Managers

### Role Summary
Vendor and Partner Managers manage third-party commitments and ensure that external contributions support project outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Track vendor scope, deliverables, milestones, dependencies, and service expectations
- Coordinate commercial, procurement, and performance escalations
- Validate third-party risks, assumptions, and acceptance evidence
- Maintain clear ownership for decisions and actions involving partners

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Report vendor status and risks to the Project Manager
- Coordinate requirements and acceptance with Product Owners and Business Analysts
- Work with Functional Leads and Technical Leads on deliverables and dependencies
- Partner with Procurement, Compliance, and the Executive Sponsor on commercial or material escalations

## Accountability and Interaction Model

The roles above should be applied according to project size, complexity, and risk. One person may perform multiple roles on a small project, but accountability for a decision should remain explicit. Higher-risk work should preserve separation of duties where practical, especially for approval, quality validation, security review, and operational acceptance.

The following principles clarify how the roles work together:

- The **Project Manager** coordinates the integrated plan, risks, dependencies, communications, and delivery cadence; they do not replace the decision authority of the Sponsor, Product Owner, Technical Lead, or Operations Owner.
- The **Product Manager or Product Owner** owns product value and priority, while the **Project Manager** owns delivery coordination and transparency. They jointly manage trade-offs between value, scope, time, and capacity.
- The **Executive Sponsor and Steering Committee** own strategic escalation and governance decisions. They should be engaged when a decision exceeds agreed project authority or materially changes investment, risk, or outcomes.
- **Business Analysts, Technical Leads, Quality Assurance Leads, Change Leads, and Operations Owners** provide specialist accountability for requirements, solution quality, adoption, and service readiness. Their evidence informs project and governance decisions.
- **Workstream Leads and Developers** own the quality and timeliness of their assigned outputs and escalate issues through the Project Manager or relevant specialist lead.
- **Release Managers** coordinate deployment, but release approval should remain with the designated Product Owner, Operations Owner, or governance authority according to the project's risk model.
- **Vendor Managers** make third-party commitments visible and ensure that external dependencies have an accountable internal owner.

### Responsibility Matrix

| Activity | Accountable | Responsible contributors | Consulted | Informed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Business case and strategic objectives | Executive Sponsor | Project Manager, Product Manager | Steering Committee, Product Owner | Delivery team |
| Product scope and priority | Product Manager or Product Owner | Business Analyst, Project Manager | Users, Technical Lead | Sponsor, delivery team |
| Integrated project plan | Project Manager | Workstream Leads, Developers | Product Owner, Technical Lead | Sponsor, stakeholders |
| Requirements and acceptance criteria | Product Owner | Business Analyst | Users, Developers, Quality Assurance Lead | Project Manager |
| Technical solution and architecture | Technical Lead or Solution Architect | Developers | Security Partner, Operations Owner | Project Manager, Product Owner |
| Risk and dependency management | Project Manager | All workstream leads | Compliance, Security, Sponsor | Steering Committee, stakeholders |
| Quality validation | Quality Assurance Lead | Testers, Developers | Product Owner, Technical Lead | Project Manager, Sponsor |
| Release readiness | Release or Deployment Manager | Quality Assurance, Technical, Operations | Product Owner, Project Manager | Sponsor, stakeholders |
| Operational handoff | Operations or Service Owner | Release Manager, Technical Lead | Product Owner, Support teams | Project Manager, Sponsor |
| Retrospective and improvement actions | Project Manager | Project team | Sponsor, Product Owner, Operations Owner | Stakeholders |

The matrix should be adapted at project initiation and revisited when scope, governance, team composition, or risk changes. Each project should record decision rights, escalation thresholds, and named individuals for the roles that are active.
